Method and sources

CarbonQuest is an educational estimator. Factors are rounded averages for India and will differ from your exact case.

  1. India grid factor about 0.71 kg CO₂ per kWh: Central Electricity Authority, CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector.
  2. Per-person emissions (India about 2 t, world about 4.7 t): Our World in Data, based on the Global Carbon Project.
  3. Per-person level consistent with a 1.5°C path by 2030, about 2.3 t: Hot or Cool Institute, 1.5-Degree Lifestyles report (2021).
  4. Transport and flight factors: UK DEFRA greenhouse gas conversion factors, adapted for Indian vehicles.
  5. Diet impact ranges: Poore and Nemecek, Science (2018); Scarborough et al., Climatic Change (2014).
  6. Climate science background: IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (2021–2023).

Limitations: no regional grid mix, no embodied emissions in housing, spending factor is a rough average. Future work: city-level data, bill upload and a mobile app.
